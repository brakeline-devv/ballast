How it works

Every asset goes in.
One clear picture comes out.

Most portfolios look diversified on the app screen: twelve tickers, a few colors. X-Ray reads the actual holdings, prices them over the last six months and shows how the account really behaves. Then it plans the fix.

1 · Your agent

Reads the holdings

Claude or Cursor reads the positions from your broker, or you paste them in the dashboard.

→
2 · X-Ray

Scores the risk

Position and sector weights, crypto share, correlations, beta, worst drop. One score from 0 to 100.

→
3 · Brakeline

Runs the fix

The rebalance orders go through Brakeline, so every single one is risk-checked before the broker.

Read-only by design

X-Ray never sends an order. It analyses and plans; execution is a separate, guarded step.

Every finding has numbers

"PLTR is 26% of the account. A 30% drop in PLTR alone costs 7.8% of everything."

Plans that respect your caps

The rebalance never pushes another position or sector over a limit to fix the first one.

What it checks

Eight things the app screen doesn't show.

Defaults follow common diversification rules. Move any limit with a slider in the dashboard and the plan updates live.

15%max
position_size

No single stock or coin above 15% of the account.

35%max
sector_exposure

Five tech names are one bet on tech. Sectors are capped as a whole.

10%max
crypto_share

BTC, ETH, SOL and crypto stocks can fall 50% in weeks. Kept in proportion.

0.7corr
moves_together

Holdings with daily correlation of 0.7 or more are grouped and weighed as one.

Nstocks
effective_positions

Twelve tickers, weighted unevenly, can behave like three. X-Ray shows the real number.

βvs S&P 500
beta

How hard the account falls when the market falls.

6months
worst_drop

The deepest fall today's holdings went through over the window.

3%cash
cash_buffer

Dry powder for a dip, so you never have to sell to buy.

Better together

X-Ray finds it.
Brakeline guards it.

Brakeline is the risk proxy between your agent and your broker: loss limits, size caps, revenge-trade blocks and a kill switch. X-Ray plans the rebalance; Brakeline makes sure every order of it is safe to send.

Plan, then approve

The agent shows you the orders and the score before and after. Nothing moves until you say so.

Checked order by order

Each trade passes Brakeline's daily loss limit, position cap and kill switch before it reaches the broker.

Practice in simulation

Apply the plan to the simulated portfolio first and watch the score drop, with zero real orders.

Developers

One MCP server. Six tools.

Node 20 or newer. Nothing to clone: npm fetches Ballast when your AI app starts it. Runs locally, so your holdings never leave your computer.

Claude Code

claude mcp add ballast -- npx -y ballast-mcp
# and the guard that executes the plan
claude mcp add brakeline -- npx -y brakeline

Claude Desktop / Cursor

// mcpServers in the config file
"ballast": {
  "command": "npx",
  "args": ["-y", "ballast-mcp"]
}

Dashboard

npx ballast-mcp dashboard
# then open localhost:6100/app
